===== PAGE 3 =====
The Need/Offer Framework
Bidirectional Marketplace
Unlike traditional platforms that separate demand 
(job boards) from supply (profiles), PMTwin treats 
both Needs and Offers as first-class collaboration 
posts.
Equal Status: A Need post ("I'm looking for...") and an 
Offer post ("I can provide...") use the same 
collaboration models.
Semantic Mirroring: Attributes are inverted, "Required 
Skills" becomes "Available Skills", "Budget" becomes 
"Rate".
Intelligent Matching: The AI engine performs 
bidirectional matching to connect compatible Needs 
with Offers.
