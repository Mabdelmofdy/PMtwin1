What do the match types mean?

Need-offer

Need posts scored against compatible offers, and offers reviewed in reverse.

Barter

Two participants each bring a need and an offer, with value equivalence for review.

Group

Role-based lead needs assembled from distinct partners with balance signals.

Cycle

Multi-party exchange chains with link scores and chain balance.
